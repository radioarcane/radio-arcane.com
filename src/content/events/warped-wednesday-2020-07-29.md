This will be the first Warped Wednesday event since late February.   Pre-COVID, Warped Wednesday was held on the last Wednesday of every month @ Seidenfaden's.

Mask up and please do not attend if feel sick in any way.   Keeping masks on unless seated will be heavily enforced.   Social distances will be required for anyone standing outside.

DJs Kaleidoscope and Sorrow-Vomit spin Darkwave, Post-Punk, Synth, Goth, EBM, Coldwave, and related styles from 10pm to close.
